## Design Context

### Users
BUSY Bar owners manage display services from their Macs and phones. They need to see the actual screen, current controller and queue, then change service access or display policy.

### Brand Personality
Compact, calm, precise. The user has chosen BUSY Cloud's dark charcoal surfaces, blue controls and Traffic Control branding.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the established shadcn/Base UI, Tailwind and TanStack stack. Preserve the approved desktop density. Mobile navigation uses an icon-only floating glass capsule inspired by Apple's current Liquid Glass guidance; other content retains the BUSY material. Motion decelerates without bounce. Keep time, preview and important state legible.

### Design Principles
- Keep live content primary and preserve its connection across navigation.
- Use stable identity and actual confirmed state; do not imply success before server acknowledgement.
- Keep configuration compact; the publisher owns exit behavior and resending.
- Fix intrinsic sizing, wrapping and touch layout instead of clipping the whole page.
- Preserve accessible names, visible focus, adequate touch targets, reduced motion and opaque material fallbacks.
